Advance Deposit / Bank Accounts

Add your JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or Bank Transfer accounts here (account holder name + account number). These appear in the "Select Deposit Method" dropdown at checkout on every product page — no coding needed, just add/edit/delete below and Save.

Shipping Fees

These fees automatically show on every product page's checkout — Standard / Express / Free shipping options.

Zeina (Ziina) Payment Gateway

Ziina lets customers pay with UAE cards, Apple Pay & Google Pay from a hosted page. Generate an access token at docs.ziina.com (Custom integration → Other builder or custom). Use test while sandbox testing, then switch to live. Ziina appears at checkout only when enabled and a token is set.

Bank App Payment

Customers pay directly from their own banking app to your bank account (card payments are currently unavailable). Enter your account details below — they appear automatically at checkout. The exact order amount is always calculated by the server, never by the customer. If your bank provides an official payment link / deep link, paste it below; otherwise customers see these details as transfer instructions. A customer's "I have paid" click never auto-verifies the payment — you confirm transfers here on the Orders page.

Online Payments Settings

Enable online payment and configure the official UAE providers you use. Only providers with valid credentials and enabled toggle appear on the customer's "Pay Online" screen — nothing is hard-coded. Ziina is configured in the Zeina card above; Bank App details in the Bank App card.

Payment Provider Status

Live status of every payment provider. Only providers marked LIVE appear on the customer's "Pay Online" screen. Providers that need a business/merchant account stay disabled until the required credentials are configured — nothing is ever faked.
